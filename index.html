The Philosophy

We cook with one ingredient every other kitchen treats as a utility.

Our hearth burns oak, applewood and grapevine cuttings from three regional orchards. Each wood is chosen for what it does to a specific ingredient — grapevine for shellfish, applewood for poultry, oak for anything that needs to stand up to a long, slow burn.

There is no walk-in freezer here. What arrives in the morning is on fire by noon and on your table by evening.
