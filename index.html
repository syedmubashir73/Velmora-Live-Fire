The Philosophy

We cook with one ingredient every other kitchen treats as a utility.

Our hearth burns oak, applewood, and grapevine cuttings from three regional orchards. Each wood is chosen specifically for its smoke profile — grapevine for shellfish, applewood for poultry, and oak for anything requiring long, sustained heat.

There is no walk-in freezer here. Fresh ingredients arriving at dawn are kissed by fire by noon and served at your table by dusk.
